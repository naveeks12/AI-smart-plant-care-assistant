Growth Timeline

Watch a plant become its best self.

Day 1 — Potting
Fresh start

Root ball settled into a pot 2 cm wider than before. Watered until it drained, then placed in bright indirect light.

Day 10 — First new leaf
Growth confirmed

Light levels and soil moisture are ideal. FloraMind keeps the watering gap at 7 days and holds fertiliser for now.

Day 30 — Feeding begins
Nutrition phase

Half-strength balanced feed introduced every 3 weeks. Health score climbed from 78% to 91%.

Day 60 — Full canopy
Peak foliage

Leaf count doubled. Rotation reminders prevented leaning and pests were caught within 48 hours.

Day 90 — Blooming / mature
Thriving

Health steady at 95%+. FloraMind moves the plant to maintenance mode: less water, monthly feed, seasonal pruning.
